What to expect

Taking the first step doesn't need to feel daunting

You don't need to have everything worked out before getting in touch. We can simply start with a conversation.

1

Get in touch

Send me a message, email or give me a call. You can share as much or as little as feels comfortable at this point.

2

Find what suits you

We can discuss whether face-to-face, online or walk-and-talk counselling is the best fit for you and arrange your session.

3

Take your time

Your sessions are your space. There's no pressure to rush, perform or know exactly what to say. We'll work at your pace.

Frequently asked questions

It's completely normal to have questions before beginning counselling.

Counselling can help you understand your thoughts and feelings, navigate challenges, develop greater self-awareness and support personal growth.

Person-centred counselling recognises that you have the ability to find your own way forward. My role is to support you while you explore what that looks like for you.

That's completely okay. You don't need to arrive with a prepared explanation. We can begin wherever you are, and you can discuss past or present difficulties whenever you feel ready.

Confidentiality is an important part of counselling. The boundaries and any exceptions to confidentiality will be discussed with you so you understand how your information is handled.

At least 24 hours' notice is required for cancellations. Late cancellations and missed appointments may incur a £40 cancellation fee.

Person-centred counselling is a therapeutic approach centred around empathy, acceptance and your individual experience.

Rather than being told what to do, you're given the space and support to better understand yourself and discover what feels right for you.

Progress looks different for everyone. It may show through greater self-awareness, improved coping skills, better communication, reduced distress, stronger confidence or simply feeling more able to understand your emotions.
