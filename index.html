Person-centred (or client-centred) therapy is a humanistic, empathetic approach developed by Carl Rogers. It places a strong emphasis on the therapeutic relationship and on your own innate capacity for growth, self-awareness and positive change.

It's effective for anxiety, depression, relationship issues, personal growth and self-esteem — often leading to greater self-awareness, better emotional regulation, and stronger communication.

Progress can be subjective, but signs to look for include increased self-awareness, improved coping skills, better communication, reduced symptoms, a stronger sense of empowerment, and a shift in perspective. I'll also check in with you regularly to gauge how things are going.
