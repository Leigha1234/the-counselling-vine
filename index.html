Frequently asked questions

Counselling can provide a wide range of benefits that help you navigate challenges, enhance your well-being, and support personal growth — the outcomes vary depending on your goals and the approach that fits you best.

You have the ability to find your own solutions through gentle exploration; I'll help you find them.

Person-centred, or client-centred, therapy is a humanistic and empathetic approach developed by Carl Rogers. It places a strong emphasis on the therapeutic relationship and your own capacity for growth, self-awareness and positive change.

It can support people experiencing anxiety, depression, relationship difficulties, low self-esteem and other personal challenges.

Progress can look different for everyone. You might notice increased self-awareness, improved coping skills, better communication, reduced distress, a stronger sense of empowerment, or simply a different perspective on what you're experiencing.

We'll address those feelings together, constructively and supportively. You'll always have a safe, non-judgemental space to express yourself openly.

Counselling is a collaborative process. If you're feeling overwhelmed, it's completely acceptable to say so and we'll work through it together.

This varies depending on the issues being addressed, your goals, your progress and the frequency of sessions. There isn't a single timeframe that is right for everyone.
